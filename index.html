Lights Trail

There's nothing quite like turning a corner and finding a whole house lit up for Christmas — kids pointing, breath fogging in the cold, that proper feeling that Christmas is nearly here. This year our neighbours have pulled out all the stops, and we've mapped every home taking part. Walk the whole trail, or just the bit near you — it's free, it's for everyone, and it raises money for our school.
